# Short-stories-by-LLM

**Turning technical ideas into stories.**

Short-stories-by-LLM is an experimental project exploring how large language models can help transform technical information, research findings, and human ideas into short fiction.

The project aims to make complex ideas easier to explore through storytelling, while creating opportunities for new perspectives, questions, and creative work.

## Why this project?

Technical knowledge is often presented in specialized documents that can be difficult to approach without prior experience.

Stories offer another way to encounter these ideas. They can place scientific discoveries, engineering challenges, and competing viewpoints in situations that readers can imagine and discuss.

This project explores how AI-assisted storytelling can help connect technical knowledge with human curiosity and creativity.

## What this project explores

- Generating short stories from technical topics and research materials.
- Using patent publications and other technical documents as sources of inspiration.
- Exploring different characters, perspectives, and fictional situations around a shared technical theme.
- Reviewing and refining AI-generated stories through human judgment and editing.

## How it works

The general workflow is:

1. Select a technical topic, research document, or creative idea.
2. Identify the concepts and questions that could inspire a story.
3. Use an LLM to generate a short story.
4. Review the result for coherence, originality, and fidelity to the source material.
5. Refine the story and record useful observations for future experiments.

The workflow and implementation may evolve as the project develops.

## Example directions

Possible experiments include:

- Fiction inspired by patent publications and emerging technologies.
- Stories exploring how scientific discoveries affect everyday life.
- Character-driven narratives about researchers, inventors, and the people who use their inventions.
- Creative interpretations of technical problems and competing approaches to solving them.

## Project status

This is an experimental, evolving project.

The repository may include source code, prompts, example stories, and development notes as they become available.
